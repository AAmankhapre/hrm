01 · Opening · all three speakers

Organizations pick the AI. Employees shape how it changes their day.

Job crafting (Wrzesniewski and Dutton, 2001) describes the self-initiated changes people make to the boundaries of their own jobs. Each lever takes a new shape in an AI-augmented consulting firm.

Show of handsAI already touches my daily work.Keep it up if I had a say in how.

Task crafting

Change what you do · Jitesh Pani

Add, drop or reshape tasks so more time goes to your strengths.

With AI: it absorbs desk research, data cleaning and first-draft decks. You spend the hours on judgment and new questions.

Relational crafting

Change who you work with · Lakshya Raj Singh Mehta

Choose the people you spend time with, so work stays human.

With AI: a new node joins your map. Use the time it saves to mentor, talk to clients and debate drafts together.

Cognitive crafting

Change how you see it · Aman Khapre

Reframe your role around its purpose, not its duty list.

With AI: move from “I produce slides” to “I help leaders make better decisions.”

05 · Relationships · Lakshya Raj Singh Mehta

Relational crafting changes who we work with, and how

Meaningful-work research (Rosso, Dekas and Wrzesniewski, 2010) names relationships and contribution to others as core sources of meaning. The question is where AI sits on your map.

1
Reinvest freed time in peopleHours saved on drafting go to client conversations, mentoring and cross-team work.
2
Define the AI relationshipTreat it as a capable but unaccountable junior analyst. Write clear briefs and verify the output.
3
Build peer learning networksShare prompts, failures and good practice so adoption is collective.

Who should deliver it?

Choose for each case, then compare with the rule.

⚠ The risk

AI crowds out learning between people.

When AI answers every question, juniors lose the chance to learn beside a senior. The safeguard is one rule: AI drafts, humans debate. Every major AI output gets one human review conversation before it reaches a client.

06 · Thinking · Aman Khapre

Cognitive crafting changes the meaning of work, not the work itself

Your task list and calendar stay as they are. What changes is how you see the purpose of the job.

Same Monday, same facts

The facts in both versions: AI now produces first-draft analysis in minutes.

The reframe ladder

The higher the rung, the more durable the meaning.

Rung 1 · Task

“I analyse data.”

Easy to automate, so easy to lose.
Rung 2 · Impact

“My analysis helps a client avoid a costly mistake.”

Ties the task to a result for someone.
Rung 3 · Purpose

“I help organizations make better-informed decisions.”

Survives changes to individual tasks.

My reframe

Reframing must be honest.

It is not denial. Some tasks and some roles will shrink. Say so, then ask what new value you can build. This is where consultants help clients most: guiding employees through the redesign, not only the tool rollout.

08 · Recommendations · all three speakers

Enable crafting, do not just deploy AI

Give people permission, time and a framework. A 90-day plan, one stage per speaker, then measurement.

  1. Days 1–30Jitesh Pani: run the task audit and the 2×2 workshop for each team.
  2. Days 31–60Lakshya Raj Singh Mehta: set up peer learning forums and the “AI drafts, humans debate” norm.
  3. Days 61–90Aman Khapre: build purpose reframing into performance talks and manager coaching.

Does job crafting protect jobs?

No. It does not guarantee a role. It gives employees agency over how their role evolves and makes their contribution easier to see.

What if the AI output is wrong?

That is why the rule is AI drafts, humans debate. Ask whether you can explain why the result is right. If you cannot, you do not own it yet.

Where does an employee start without a mandate?

Inside their own scope. Audit your own week, try one change for two weeks, tell your manager what you changed and why, then review it.
